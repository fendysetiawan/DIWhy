DIWHY

General Game Narrative Description: 
In a world of generative AI solutions, one homeowner decides to leave the professional behind and do it themselves.

Gameplay:
The user buys a house, and they decide they want to undergo DIY projects in the house. The user calls the contractor to ask for a quote, then they decide they can do it themselves. The user’s baseline is the contractor’s budget and timeline.
The projects are:
Creating an open-floor plan by removing a wall.
Fixing a leaking pipe in the bathroom.
Replacing the problematic dryer.
Each of these problems opens up randomizes new problems.
Tearing down the wall and creating the open-floor plan cuts through electric cables, or leads to collapse of part of the slab if the user decides to cut a big part of the wall. It can also lead to a mold in the wall.
Fixing the leaking pipe leads to breaking the flooring, or destroying the electric circuit of the bathroom.
Replacing the dryer on smoke needs cleaning up the dust behind it, and possibly taking down the wall next to it. Taking down the wall might show a beehive.

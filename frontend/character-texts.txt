DIWhy: character texts
======================
40 text messages per character. The game draws from these at random.
Lines starting with a number are the texts. {days} and {price} are filled in by the game.

Casting: every game, the 7 sprites are shuffled. The player picks one sprite as the Home Owner;
until they do, the Home Owner is random too. The other six are dealt out to the NPC roles at random.

## PAYMENTS: bank fraud alert flow (in the code as pay(); keep it when the code is combined)
Whenever the player pays for anything (finishing a task with a cost, or buying from the Store Clerk):
- The payment is put ON HOLD. The Budget line on the post-it shows "$... on hold".
- The BANK (an extra contact with a bank icon) texts: "Potential fraud: a payment of $X at the hardware store (item) is on hold. Was this you?"
- The STORE CLERK texts that the card is not going through.
- The player answers the bank: "Yes, it was me." (the money leaves the budget) or "No, block it." (red, the payment is cancelled).
- Bank and clerk then confirm what happened.

Clerk, card declined:
- Hm, your card isn't going through. Want to try another one? We also sell gift cards.
- Card declined. Happens all the time. Mostly to people buying this much grout.
- The machine says no. I say: have you seen our payment plans?
- Your card got rejected. I'll keep your cart warm. And add a few things.
- Card not going through. Did your bank see your project?
Clerk, after "Yes, it was me":
- It went through! Receipt's in the bag. Also, have you seen our new cordless everything?
- Approved! See you in about an hour.
- Payment worked. Your bank must believe in you. I do too. Mostly.
Clerk, after "No, block it":
- Still declined. I put your stuff behind the counter. It misses you already.
- No luck. Cash works. So does a second card. So does a third.


## CRACKED PHONE (in the code as crackPhone())
After a big fix (a task costing $300 or more, or an urgent one), there is a 1 in 10 chance the phone gets a small crack
in the lower left corner of the screen. The text stays readable. Add ?crack to the address to see it straight away.
